Two chains of offices run side by side

The administration (Commissioner, Deputy Commissioner, Assistant Commissioner) handles revenue, land and law and order. Elected local government handles civic services and civil registration. Its lowest tier is the Union Council in most of Pakistan and the Village or Neighbourhood Council in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Union Councils were replaced by 2,996 Village Councils (rural) and 505 Neighbourhood Councils (urban). Tehsil chairmen, and the Mayor in Peshawar, are directly elected.

Punjab

A new Local Government Act took effect in 2025. Delimitation finished in August 2026: each Union Council covers roughly 22,000 to 31,000 people and has a 13-member house. Lahore alone has 433.

Sindh

Districts are split into talukas. Rural areas have Union Councils; in Karachi the tier is called a Union Committee, with 246 across 25 towns under the city council.

Balochistan, Islamabad, AJK, GB

Union Councils are the lowest tier. Islamabad has 125. Balochistan regrouped its map in July 2026 into 11 divisions, so some district assignments are still settling.

What do I get done at each level?

The usual first stop for common tasks. Procedures differ between provinces and districts, so check the full Sahulat guide for the task before you go.

Union / Village / Neighbourhood Council

Life events

  • Birth registration and certificate
  • Death registration and certificate
  • Marriage (nikah) registration
  • Divorce registration and reconciliation notice

Ask for the council Secretary. Certificates are computerised through NADRA's civil registration system.

Tehsil / Taluka

Land and municipal services

  • Fard and intiqal from the patwari or land record centre
  • Building plan approval and NOCs
  • Water, sanitation and streetlight complaints

Assistant Commissioner's office for revenue; the tehsil or town municipal office for civic services.

District

Certificates and complaints

  • Domicile certificate
  • Price control and overcharging complaints
  • District grievances and coordination with police, health and education

Deputy Commissioner's office at the district headquarters.

District election office

Which Union Council am I in?

  • Official Union Council list and boundaries (delimitation)
  • Voter list for your area

Phone numbers are in the directory where the Election Commission publishes them.

Not by area

Federal identity services

  • CNIC and B-Form
  • Family Registration Certificate (FRC)
  • Passport

Any NADRA or Passport office, not only in your own district.

About this data. Divisions, districts and tehsils for all four provinces, Islamabad, Azad Jammu & Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan, checked on 6 October 2026. Population is Census 2023 (AJK 2017). New tehsils carved out after the census show a dash; their people are still counted in the parent tehsil. Where sources disagree, the district figure is the total of its tehsils. Balochistan follows the July 2026 notification and its tehsil assignments are provisional.
About phone numbers. Only numbers published by the Election Commission of Pakistan or NADRA are shown. Individual Union Councils do not publish phone numbers, so none are listed; your district election office or the council Secretary at the council office can confirm your UC. The Punjab district list comes from an ECP directory made before the newest districts were created, so those districts point to the parent office. Call ahead, as numbers change.
