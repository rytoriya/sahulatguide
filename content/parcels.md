## Volumetric (size) weight

Couriers charge the **higher** of actual weight and size weight:

> **Size weight (kg) = Length × Width × Height (cm) ÷ 5000** (some domestic / economy services use ÷ 6000)

| Box | Size (cm) | Size weight |
|---|---|---|
| Flyer | 25×18×2 | 0.2 kg |
| Small | 30×20×10 | 1.2 kg |
| Medium | 40×30×20 | 4.8 kg |
| Large | 50×40×30 | 12 kg |
| Extra large | 60×50×40 | 24 kg |

Tip: Use the smallest box that fits, or a courier flyer for soft items like clothes.

## How to pack

### General rules (every parcel)

1. Use a strong, new-looking box. Old boxes lose strength.
2. Fill empty space with paper or bubble wrap so nothing moves when shaken.
3. Tape all seams in an 'H' pattern with wide packing tape.
4. Write receiver's name, full address, city and TWO phone numbers clearly; add your own as sender.
5. Remove or cover old labels and barcodes.
6. Take a photo of the item and the packed box. Useful for claims.

### Fragile items

1. Wrap each item separately in 2-3 layers of bubble wrap.
2. Stand plates and glasses on their edge, not flat.
3. Leave 5 cm of padding on every side; use a double box for glass.
4. Mark FRAGILE and THIS WAY UP on all sides.
5. Buy insurance and declare the real value.

### Clothes & textiles

1. Fold and put in a plastic bag first (protects from rain).
2. A courier flyer is cheapest for 1-2 kg, no box needed.
3. Vacuum bags reduce size and volumetric weight.
4. Abroad: list each item and value on the customs form.

### Food & mithai

1. Use airtight, sealed containers; tape the lids.
2. Double-bag in zip-lock bags to stop leaks and smell.
3. Send by overnight/same-day only; avoid weekends.
4. Abroad: only sealed, labelled, shop-bought food is safe; avoid meat and dairy.

### Phones & electronics

1. Back up and switch the device off; keep the battery inside.
2. Use the original box if possible, inside a bigger padded box.
3. Remove SIM and memory cards.
4. Note the serial/IMEI number and declare the value; buy insurance.

### Perfume & liquids

1. Tape the cap, then wrap in cling film.
2. Put in a sealed zip-lock bag with tissue to absorb leaks.
3. Declare it. Undeclared perfume can be seized at the airport.
4. Within Pakistan, ask for road (overland) service.

### Documents

1. Use a plastic sleeve, then a stiff envelope or courier flyer.
2. Add a card sheet so certificates don't bend.
3. Keep photocopies of everything you send.
4. For originals (degrees, passports) use tracked express with insurance.
