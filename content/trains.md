## 📱 How to book

**Online with RABTA (official Pakistan Railways app)**

1. Install RABTA — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.easyway.ticket.app) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/pk/app/rabta/id6446243993) — and register with your CNIC and mobile number.
2. Choose **Train Tickets**, enter From, To and date.
3. Pick your train and class, then seats/berths for each passenger.
4. Pay by debit/credit card, JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank app.
5. Show the e-ticket with your original CNIC on the train.

**At the station:** visit the reservation office with your original CNIC; unreserved Economy tickets can be bought shortly before departure (no guaranteed seat).

**Cancelling:** refunds are possible before departure with a deduction that grows closer to departure time — cancel as early as you can.

**Helpline:** 117 · [pakrail.gov.pk](https://www.pakrail.gov.pk/)

## 💡 Tips to pay less & travel better

- **Cheapest seat:** Economy on an ordinary express (Awam, Khyber Mail, Allama Iqbal, Fareed).
- **Fastest:** Green Line, Karakoram, Shah Hussain and Sir Syed skip small stations; Lahore–Rawalpindi takes ~4.5–5 h on Subak Raftar / Subak Kharam / Islamabad Express.
- **Overnight trains** save a hotel night — pick AC Sleeper or Business berths.
- **Book early** for Eid and holidays.
- **Carry your original CNIC** (B-Form for children); arrive 30–45 minutes early.
- **Check live running status** in RABTA before leaving home — delays are common.
