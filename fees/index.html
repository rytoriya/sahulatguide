Pay school, college and university fees online

Most fee vouchers now carry a 1Bill or Kuickpay number. Pay it from any bank app, JazzCash or Easypaisa in a minute, with no queue at the bank. Search below to check whether your institution is set up.

How to pay a fee voucher from your phone

Look at your fee voucher or challan first. It will show which of these numbers to use.

1Bill (1Link)

Voucher shows a 1Bill, invoice or consumer number

  1. Open your bank app, JazzCash or Easypaisa.
  2. Go to Bill Payment and choose 1Bill → Invoice / Voucher. Some apps, such as HBL's, also have an Education option.
  3. Enter the number from the voucher. Some boards and universities ask you to add a fixed prefix before the challan number (see the table below).
  4. Check that the institution's name and amount are correct, then pay.
  5. Save the receipt or transaction ID (TID).

Also works at any 1Link ATM and over the counter at most banks.

Kuickpay

Voucher shows a Kuickpay ID

  1. Open your bank app or wallet and go to Bill Payment.
  2. Choose Kuickpay (sometimes listed under Education or Other).
  3. Enter the Kuickpay ID or consumer number from the voucher.
  4. Check the name and amount, then pay.
  5. Save the receipt. You can also pay by debit or credit card on Kuickpay's own site.

Also payable at ATMs and bank counters.

Bank challan only

Voucher shows only a bank account

  1. Pay at a branch of the bank printed on the challan, or check whether the institution's portal offers an online option.
  2. Ask the accounts office whether a 1Bill or Kuickpay number is available; many institutions added one recently.
  3. Never transfer fees to a personal account.

Government school fees are usually free or collected at the school.
