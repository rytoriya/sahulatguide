        💡 Good to know

        • Pakistani passports are only for Pakistani citizens (including dual nationals). Visitors use a visa, or a POC / NICOP card if eligible — both remove the need for a visa.
        • The free "Visa Prior to Arrival" ended on 1 Jan 2026. Online visas now take about 7–10 business days and the fee depends on nationality.
        • Your passport should be valid for at least 6 months beyond your stay.
