Where these rates come from

Indicative rates only. Fuel prices are the government-notified maximum retail prices. Gold and silver are Sarafa association rates; jewellers add making charges. Banks and exchange companies set their own currency rates. Share prices are refreshed hourly in market hours; use the PSX data portal for live prices.
Rates are read from data/rates.json and data/stocks.json on this site and checked again every 5 minutes while this page is open.
