How admission works

  1. Pick your field, then your test. Medical and dental: MDCAT (every college). Engineering: ECAT (Punjab), ETEA (KP), NET (NUST), NED or MUET tests (Sindh), PIEAS test. General BS: the university's own test, HEC USAT or NTS NAT. Law: HEC LAT.
  2. Check you're eligible before you pay. MBBS/BDS needs at least 65% in FSc Pre-Medical. Engineering needs 60% in FSc Pre-Engineering. Most BS programmes ask for 45–50% in Intermediate.
  3. Register for the test online on the official site and pay the test fee by bank challan, 1Bill or card. Keep your roll-number slip.
  4. Sit the test, then apply to each university. Passing the test isn't admission: you still apply to each university by its own deadline and select the programmes you want.
  5. Watch the merit lists. Your aggregate (see the formulas) decides your place. Most universities publish several lists, so a place can open up later.
  6. Pay the fee voucher by the due date or the seat goes to the next student. Many universities take 1Bill or Kuickpay: how to pay fees online.

Money help

  • Benazir Undergraduate Scholarship (HEC): tuition and stipend for students from low-income families.
  • HEC Need-Based Scholarship: apply through your university's financial aid office.
  • Most universities also waive or reduce fees for hafiz-e-Quran, disabled students and children of employees; check the prospectus.
